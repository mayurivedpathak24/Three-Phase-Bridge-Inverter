Three-Phase Bridge Inverter
📌 Project Overview
A Three-Phase Bridge Inverter is a power electronic circuit that converts DC power into three-phase AC power. This project focuses on understanding the working of inverter switches, switching sequences, and generation of a three-phase AC output.
🎯 Objectives
To study the working principle of a three-phase bridge inverter.
To understand DC-to-AC power conversion.
To study the operation of power electronic switching devices.
To understand the switching sequence for generating three-phase output.
To study the applications of inverters in electrical systems.
⚙️ Components Used
DC power supply
Power electronic switches (IGBT/MOSFET)
Gate pulse/control circuit
Three-phase load
Connecting wires
Measuring instruments
Protection components
🔧 Working Principle
The inverter uses six power electronic switches arranged in a three-phase bridge configuration. The switches are controlled using appropriate gate pulses.
By switching the devices in a proper sequence, the DC input is converted into a three-phase AC output. The switching pattern determines the output voltage and frequency.
🔄 Power Conversion
DC Supply → Three-Phase Bridge Inverter → Three-Phase AC Output → Load
